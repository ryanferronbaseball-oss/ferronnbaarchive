THE SCIENCE OF DOING NOTHING

Cardio Score · Version 2.0

Only players scoring exactly 0 points with at least 15:00 (college) or 18:00 (NBA) are eligible. Automatic eliminators: 6+ rebounds, 4+ assists, 3+ stocks (steals + blocks), or 7+ field-goal attempts. The maximum is 100 points.

Statistical inactivity (70 points): begin with 10 points in each of seven categories. Subtract 7 per FGA, 7 per FTA, 6 per rebound, 7 per assist, 8 per steal, 8 per block and 5 per turnover. Each category bottoms out at zero.

Minutes (30 points): 0.75 × minutes, capped at 30. Exact source minutes are used where available. Missing stats never count as zero.

Induction status: the official Hall of Fame score cutoff has not yet been chosen. This is a qualifying leaderboard, not a prematurely inducted class.

Available NBA and college data are evaluated independently and deduplicated by league, game and player. This page reflects the imported box scores currently in the archive; missing or unverified rows are excluded rather than assumed to be empty.
