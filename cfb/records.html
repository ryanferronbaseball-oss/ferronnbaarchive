About these records

Only games attended are included. Missing source values never count as zero. Quarter, comeback, and lead-change records are calculated from verified final scores and archived scoring events. Full-PPR uses traditional ESPN-style offensive scoring, with no IDP or kicker fantasy points. A play is credited to a named player only when the underlying game report identifies that player. Some older defensive statistics may be incomplete.
