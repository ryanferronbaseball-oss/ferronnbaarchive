The standard for inclusion

A team must be in the archive as an attended opponent or home team during the same season it won the NBA title, World Series, Super Bowl, or a comparable top-level national championship. Conference winners, runners-up, semifinalists, minor honors, and championships from other seasons do not qualify. A champion's entry links only to games you actually attended.
