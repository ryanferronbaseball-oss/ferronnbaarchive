How highlights are selected

Overtime, comeback and margin records use verified game and scoring data. A comeback is the largest recorded scoring deficit overcome by the eventual winner; scoring timelines may not include every non-scoring play. Game-winning touchdown refers to the final touchdown that established the winning lead, not necessarily the final scoring play.

For all scoring plays, visit Touchdown History. For single-game statistical leaders, visit Records & Milestones.
