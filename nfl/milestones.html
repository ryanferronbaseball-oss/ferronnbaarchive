How Full-PPR is calculated

Traditional ESPN-style full-PPR offense: one point per catch, four for passing touchdowns and six for rushing or receiving touchdowns. No bonus categories or TE premium.

+0.04 per yardPassing
+4Passing touchdown
−2Interception thrown
+0.1 per yardRushing or receiving
+6Rushing or receiving TD
+1Each reception
−2Fumble lost
+2Successful offensive 2-point conversion
+6Individual kickoff/punt return TD

These particular games had no successful offensive two-point conversions and no credited individual kickoff/punt return touchdowns in the imported category rows. Defensive two-point scores are not offensive PPR points. Kicker, D/ST and individual-defender fantasy scoring are separate systems and deliberately excluded; compare those players using the existing Defense and Special Teams leaderboards.
