How records are counted

Individual records are single-game highs, drawn from ESPN's imported box-score categories. Team and game records use the final scores and quarter scoring of the attended games. Ties are displayed together.

Only games you attended are included—not players' career or season records. Missing source categories are not interpreted as zero. For kickers, made field goals and longest successful kicks are derived from source kicking lines. QB rating and percentage fields are not used as cumulative records. Sack totals and other defensive categories may have incomplete historical coverage.
