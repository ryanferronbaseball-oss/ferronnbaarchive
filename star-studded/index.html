THE METHODOLOGY

Star power, not game quality.

Only players who appeared

Each individual is counted once per game, even if they earned multiple awards or appear in multiple statistical categories. Backup players who never entered the game do not count.

Career legacy with hindsight

Verified Hall of Fame status, MVPs, major individual honors, All-Star or All-League recognition and college-to-pro achievements inform the ranking. Awards may have been earned before or after the game you attended.

Depth first, prestige second

The Star Power Index begins with the number of distinct recognized players, then adds weight for the highest honors. It is a descriptive archive index, not an official award or a judgment about the game's quality.

Coverage is limited to sports with linked player appearances and verified achievement files. An athlete absent from this list is not necessarily unaccomplished; some historical honors or game rosters are still being researched. Rankings refresh as the underlying datasets are improved.
